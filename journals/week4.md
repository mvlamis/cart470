There is the sound of a very faint but blood-curdling scream of terror playing in my mind as I'm writing this journal, but it's mostly covered up by the sounds of a beautiful website that I can see approaching in the distance. 

In our weekly meeting with Sabine, the main takeaway was that we should be solidifying our ideas of what the final goal is by approaching VK Preston with some concrete, visual concepts that they can pick from or select aspects that they want to incorporate. We also threw around some ideas about the structure and framework itself, like making it dynamic and evolving or gameified. After the meeting, I think we all felt pretty motivated and ready to get started. If only this was the end of the story.

We spoke to the magical wise one (VK) once more this week in search of answers. After following the yellow brick road, we arrived at the visualization studio, and after some technical difficulties, we were excited to finally learn why exactly we were there. We are still wondering. Regardless of the superfluous field trip, we still wanted to further discuss the project and raise some questions. VK is a very kind and talented person who clearly cares deeply about their art. I'm suspecting that the reason for some of their hollow verbosity is that they were thrown into this process equally as abruptly as we are, and that we're on a much more level playing field than I initially thought. This means that we have a lot more agency and freedom to take initiative. 

The question of hosting was the most tangibly-answered out of all of them even though we don't actually have an answer. It's good that we brought it up now instead of later because they predict that there could be a long chain of contacts to reach out to to figure it out. The good news is that it's unlikely to be running on the Concordia servers, so the risk of PHP is less likely.

Even through the aforementioned flops, I still feel optimistic about how this project is going to turn out, and this week did provide some useful guidance on our next steps.
